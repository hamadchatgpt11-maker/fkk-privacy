Privacy Policy

App: Faisal Kutub Khana (package: com.faisalkutubkhana.reader)
Last updated: 16 November 2026

Faisal Kutub Khana ("the app", "we") is an Android app for reading Pashto, Urdu and Arabic Islamic books, with prayer times, Qibla direction, duas, tasbih, notes and tasks. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your own phone and is not sent to us:

  • Your settings (language, theme, widget style, reading options).
  • Your reading progress, bookmarks and favourite books.
  • Your notes, daily tasks and tasbih counts.
  • The city or location you choose for prayer times.

You can remove all of this at any time from your phone's Settings by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Books and internet use

To read a book, the app downloads the book file from our server over the internet. The download request contains only the book you opened. We do not link downloads to any person.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: to download books.
  • Notifications: to show prayer-time reminders and play the azan tune.
  • Exact alarms / boot completed: so that prayer-time reminders go off on time, even after the phone restarts.
  • List of launchable apps: used only inside the "Watch and apps" home-screen widget, so that you can choose apps (such as Facebook or YouTube) to show next to the clock. The list is read on your device, is not stored by us and is never uploaded.
  • Sensors (compass): to show the Qibla direction on your phone.

5. Screen capture

While a book is open, the app blocks screenshots and screen recording to protect the books from copying.

6. Third parties

We do not use advertising services and do not share data with third parties. The home-screen widgets and notifications are provided by the Android system.

7. Children

The app is a general Islamic reading app. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: hamadskhan88@gmail.com
